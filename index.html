Our Story

Dharavi-born.
Mumbai-raised.
World-class.

"Food is how Mumbai says I love you."— Suresh Malvankar, Founder

Rasa began in a small kitchen in Dharavi in 2003, with Suresh Malvankar's grandmother's recipes and a dream to bring authentic Indian flavours to every corner of Mumbai. Two decades on, we've catered over 8,000 events across the city.

From intimate home pujas in Dadar to grand weddings at the Taj Lands End, we cook with the same love — local ingredients, traditional methods, and an unwavering commitment to making every guest feel like family.

Our team of 60 chefs and catering professionals serve every cuisine Mumbai loves: Maharashtrian, Mughlai, South Indian, Continental, and everything in between.
